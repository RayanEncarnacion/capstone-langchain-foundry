# Study Session and Break Policy

Northstar uses short, deliberate sessions to make progress easier to start and measure.

## Standard session

The standard study session is **25 minutes of focused work followed by a 5-minute break**. A learner may combine two standard sessions into an extended block of **50 minutes followed by a 10-minute break**.

The 25-minute duration is a default, not a test requirement. Learners may record another preferred duration, but the assistant should not silently change the preference based on a single unusually long or short session.

## Session contents

Every focused session should contain one clear objective. Good objectives include:

- Explain a concept without consulting notes.
- Complete three practice questions.
- Compare two approaches and record the trade-offs.
- Correct mistakes from the previous assessment.

“Study the whole module” is too broad for one session.

## Breaks

A break is intended to interrupt continuous cognitive effort. Learners should stand, stretch, drink water, or look away from the screen. Starting another demanding task does not count as a break.

After four standard sessions, the recommended long break is **20 to 30 minutes**.

## Ending a session

At the end of a session, record:

1. What was completed.
2. One point that remains unclear.
3. The next smallest useful action.

This short record is an activity log, not long-term user memory. The assistant should store it with the relevant task or session rather than treating every observation as a permanent preference.

## Interruptions

If a session is interrupted for more than ten minutes, close it and begin a new session later. Do not combine separated periods into one session merely to make the total duration look larger.

## Accessibility

Learners may use shorter sessions, longer breaks, text-to-speech, captions, reduced-distraction formats, or another reasonable accommodation. Assessment scoring does not change because a learner uses an accommodation.
