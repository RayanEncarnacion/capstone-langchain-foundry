# Assessment and Progress Policy

Assessments in Northstar are feedback mechanisms. Their purpose is to expose gaps and guide the next practice task.

## Diagnostic

The initial diagnostic contains 15 questions and does not count toward completion. Its score helps select an initial learning track. A learner may retake the diagnostic after completing at least two weeks, but the original result remains in the progress history.

## Module checkpoints

Each module ends with a checkpoint containing a mixture of recall, explanation, and application questions. The current passing score is **80 percent**.

A learner may make **three attempts per checkpoint**. After a second unsuccessful attempt, the learner must wait **24 hours** before the third attempt. During that interval, the recommended action is to review incorrect answers and complete one targeted practice task.

The highest score is shown as the current result, while all attempts remain in the history.

## Completion

A six-week cycle is complete when the learner has:

- Passed every required module checkpoint.
- Completed a final application exercise.
- Written a short review identifying one strength, one remaining gap, and one next step.

Attendance at live sessions is optional and does not affect completion.

## Progress status

Progress uses four labels:

- **Not started** — no activity has been recorded.
- **In progress** — work has begun but requirements remain.
- **Ready for review** — required tasks are done and the learner should take the checkpoint.
- **Complete** — the checkpoint and application requirements are satisfied.

## Feedback

Feedback should point to the relevant concept or source rather than simply reporting a score. When an answer is wrong, the learner should be told what type of misunderstanding occurred: missing fact, confused distinction, incorrect process, or unsupported conclusion.

The assistant must not claim that a learner passed unless an actual recorded assessment result meets the current 80 percent threshold.
