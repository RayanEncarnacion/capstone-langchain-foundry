# Northstar Participant Handbook 2024

> **Archive notice:** This handbook describes the 2024 version of the fictional Northstar program. It is retained for historical questions and must not be used as the current policy.

## Historical program format

In 2024, the standard Northstar cycle lasted **eight weeks**. Cohorts began once per quarter and learners were expected to remain aligned with their assigned cohort.

The program used two stages: guided instruction followed by independent practice. The three-stage foundation, applied practice, and review model had not yet been adopted.

## Historical study sessions

The recommended study block was **45 minutes followed by a 15-minute break**. Learners were encouraged to complete at least one block every weekday.

This recommendation was replaced after the 2025 pilot found that shorter default sessions were easier to schedule consistently.

## Historical assessment policy

The passing checkpoint score was **70 percent**. Learners could retry a checkpoint without a fixed attempt limit, although facilitators could require additional practice before another attempt.

The current program no longer uses this rule.

## Historical live schedule

The weekly question session took place on **Thursday at 15:00 UTC**, and the cohort review was held on Monday at 17:00 UTC.

These event times are obsolete. Refer to a current schedule document for present-day events.

## Historical task guidance

The handbook suggested maintaining up to ten open actions. Task statuses were `open` and `closed`; the current `planned`, `in_progress`, and `done` statuses were introduced later.

## Correct use of this document

This source can answer questions such as:

- How long was a Northstar cycle in 2024?
- What score passed a checkpoint under the 2024 rules?
- When did the old weekly question session occur?

It must not answer questions such as “What score do I need now?” unless the response clearly contrasts the historical policy with a current official source.
